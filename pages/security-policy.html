Security Policy

Effective: October 2026

At Jagvix, we take the security of our systems and our clients' data seriously. This policy outlines our security practices and how to report vulnerabilities responsibly.

1. Our Commitment to Security

  • All data transmitted to and from our website is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • We implement strict Content Security Policies (CSP) to prevent cross-site scripting attacks.
  • Form inputs are sanitised to prevent injection attacks.
  • We follow the principle of least privilege for all system access.
  • Regular security reviews and updates are performed on our infrastructure.

2. Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly.

Email: security@jagvix.com

Please include a clear description of the vulnerability, steps to reproduce it, and any supporting evidence (screenshots, logs). Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability until we have had an opportunity to address it.

3. Response Timeline

  • Acknowledgement: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours.
  • Assessment: We will assess the severity and impact within 5 business days.
  • Resolution: We aim to resolve critical vulnerabilities within 15 business days.
  • Updates: We will keep you informed of our progress throughout the process.

4. Data Protection Measures

  • Encryption: HTTPS/TLS for all data in transit. Sensitive data encrypted at rest where applicable.
  • Access Controls: Role-based access controls with multi-factor authentication for internal systems.
  • Monitoring: Automated monitoring for unusual activity and potential threats.
  • Backups: Regular encrypted backups with tested recovery procedures.
  • Vendor Security: Third-party service providers are evaluated for security compliance.

5. Scope

This security policy covers:

  • The Jagvix website (jagvix.com) and all its subdomains.
  • Web applications and services operated by Jagvix.
  • APIs and integrations managed by Jagvix.

This policy does not cover third-party services, platforms or tools linked from our website.

6. Safe Harbour

We consider security research conducted in accordance with this policy to be authorised. We will not pursue legal action against researchers who report vulnerabilities responsibly and in good faith.
